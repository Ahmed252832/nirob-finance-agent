# Talk to Nirob — version 3.1

## Try a conversation

1. Open `START_NIROB.cmd`, wait for the local model, then unlock with your existing passkey.
2. Choose **Agent**. The workspace now opens **Talk to Nirob** with a message box and conversation history.
3. Ask: **“Suggest a small business idea after analysing my previous months of spending.”**
4. Continue: **“I am good at mathematics, have 5 hours a week, and can spend at most 3000 taka.”**
5. Ask: **“Give me a practical first-week plan for that idea.”**

Press Enter to send; Shift+Enter adds a new line. **New chat** starts a separate conversation. Use the conversation selector to return to an earlier one. **Copy** copies an answer. A waiting indicator stays visible during local generation; there is no token-by-token streaming in this version.

## What the answer uses

Nirob reads up to six recorded months of posted income and expenses, current unpaid bills, savings goals and category totals. Open **Financial context used** below an answer to inspect its computed figures. The current month is labelled partial. Missing months are unknown, never assumed to have zero spending.

The supplied dataset currently contains one month. Nirob can therefore suggest a cautious experiment using that month's records and your stated skills, but cannot honestly compare earlier months yet. As posted history accumulates, it is included automatically. This update does not add bank syncing or a historical-statement importer. Unrecorded expenses can make affordability estimates incomplete.

Suggestions are generated by a local model using recent conversation messages. It can ask follow-up questions and adjust an idea to your stated skills, available time and budget. It does not prove demand, validate current prices, guarantee income or replace professional financial advice. Conversation context is bounded; restate important constraints in long chats. It is not equivalent to ChatGPT in knowledge or reasoning quality.

## Payments remain separate

An explicit **“Send 500 to Rahim”** or **“Pay my electricity bill”** prepares a draft. Use **Review payment**, check the details and approve with your passkey. The model cannot execute it. “Yes, do it” in an advice conversation does not authorize a payment. Every final simulated execution still passes the Guardian and verified transaction-bound approval.

All accounts and payments remain simulated. No bank is connected. Chat records stay in the local database; protect that database and private backups.
