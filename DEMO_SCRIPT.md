## Recorded flow

1. Open the complete application folder and launch `START_NIROB.cmd`; register/unlock a passkey.
2. Ask in chat for a maths-tutoring business idea with three hours per week and a BDT 2,000 ceiling.
3. Expand the seven completed synthetic months plus current-month context; ask a first-week-plan follow-up.
4. Calculate/save a budget and extract/review a clean sample bill image.
5. Ask `Send 500 to Rahim`; inspect the pending draft.
6. Rate the draft, separately review the exact payment, then approve with a fresh passkey.
7. Show the posted simulated expense and add a cash purchase.
8. Generate and block an unknown demo request.
9. Explain the 100-case benchmark: 100 passes, 96.30% aggregate lower bound, 95% requirement, 80 model cases plus 20 deterministic checks. Personal trust is separate.
10. Rename the workspace, add a custom demo entry, preview/import a JSON dataset.
11. Demonstrate clear-records and full-reset confirmation in the recording workspace only.

Current user data was not used or reset. The source package never includes recording credentials, model service keys, private app data or browser profiles.
