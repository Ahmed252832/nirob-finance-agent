Recheck organizer announcements before submitting. This is our mapping, not organizer approval.

## Category

**Topic A** is the appropriate proposed category: personal finance, smart budgeting, bill analysis, spending insights and actionable income/expense advice. The prototype demonstrates the AI agent with permitted tools and synthetic-account execution logs. Real bank feeds are not necessary for this demonstration under the non-on-chain evidence provisions. This does not waive the broader submission and eligibility conditions.

No blockchain, testnet or real transaction hashes are implemented. Therefore Topic F's real on-chain hash requirement is not met. Topic C would need a genuine international-student/cross-border-finance focus or organizer clarification; simply being a Bangladeshi team is insufficient. No sweeping claim is made that blockchain does not work in Bangladesh.

## Evidence by scoring area

* Task completion (40): English app, local model traces, bill extraction, budgets, ledger and execution video.
* Security/compliance (30): server-verified passkeys, transaction binding, Guardian, audit chain and candid security self-assessment. All money remains simulated; live PSP/bank use requires provider authorization and an approved integration.
* Innovation (30): scope-specific earned preparation autonomy with a user-controlled final approval boundary. Innovation score is the judges' decision.
